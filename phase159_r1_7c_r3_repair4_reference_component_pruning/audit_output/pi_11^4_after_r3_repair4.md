# Group proof narrative

## 証明対象

\[
\pi_{11}^{4} = 0.
\]

## 使用する結果

**[R1] Proposition 5.15.**
$\pi_{10}^{3} = 0$.
**[R2] Proposition 5.8.**
$\pi_{n + 4}^{n} = 0$, $n \ge 6$ が成り立つ.
**[R3] Proposition 4.4.**
$(α, \beta) \mapsto Eα + \nu_{4}\beta: \pi_{i - 1}^{3} \oplus \pi_{i}^{7} \to \pi_{i}^{4}$ は同型.
---

## 証明

[R1] より, $\pi_{10}^{3} = 0$.

[R2] より, $\pi_{11}^{7} = 0$.

[R3] より, $\pi_{10}^{3} \oplus \pi_{11}^{7} \xrightarrow{\cong} \pi_{11}^{4}$.

以上より, $\pi_{11}^{4} = 0$.
