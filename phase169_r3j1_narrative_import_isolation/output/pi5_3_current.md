証明

$\pi_{5}^{3}$の群構造を決定するために, [R5]を用いて次の完全列を考える.

$\pi_{6}^{3} \xrightarrow{H} \pi_{6}^{5} \xrightarrow{\Delta} \pi_{4}^{2} \xrightarrow{E} \pi_{5}^{3} \xrightarrow{H} \pi_{5}^{5} \xrightarrow{\Delta} \pi_{3}^{2}$.

[R1]より, $\pi_{4}^{3} = \mathbb{Z}/2\{\eta_{3}\}$.

[R3]より, $\pi_{4}^{2} = \mathbb{Z}/2\{\eta_{2}^{2}\}$.

[R2]を用いて, $\pi_{4}^{2} = \mathbb{Z}/2\{\eta_{2}^{2}\}$.

[R4]より, $H\left(\nu'\right) = \eta_{5}$.

$H: \pi_{6}^{3} \to \pi_{6}^{5} \text{ is surjective}$.

完全性より, $\operatorname{Im}\Delta=\ker E=0$ なので, $\Delta: \pi_{6}^{5}\to \pi_{4}^{2}$ は零写像.

$E: \pi_{4}^{2} \to \pi_{5}^{3} \text{ is injective}$.

$\Delta: \pi_{5}^{5} \to \pi_{3}^{2} \text{ is injective}$.

$H: \pi_{5}^{3} \to \pi_{5}^{5} \text{ is the zero map}$.

$E: \pi_{4}^{2} \to \pi_{5}^{3} \text{ is surjective}$.

$E: \pi_{4}^{2}\to \pi_{5}^{3}$ は同型.

[R6]より, $E(\eta_{2})=\eta_{3}$.

[R6]より, $E(\eta_{3})=\eta_{4}$.

以上より, $\pi_{5}^{3} = \mathbb{Z}/2\{\eta_{3}^{2}\}$.
